I don’t know what I’m actually facing yet.

That is normal, and it is what the case review is for. Bring what you have — the citation, the paperwork, your own account — and you will leave the first call knowing what you are facing and what happens next.

Should I talk to the police first?

No. You have the right to remain silent and the right to counsel, and using them is not evidence of anything. Let a defense attorney deal with investigators so you do not have to.
